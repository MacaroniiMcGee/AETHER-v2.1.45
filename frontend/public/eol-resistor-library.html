Dual-EOL Wiring Library

24 supervised-input configurations from access control and intrusion panels. Every card shows where each resistor goes and the loop resistance the panel should see in each state. In each pair the first value is the series EOL resistor and the second is the alarm resistor across the contact, so "1k/1k" reads normal 1 kΩ and alarm 2 kΩ.

Fit at the device

Both resistors go at the far end, inside the contact or REX housing. Resistors fitted at the panel terminals supervise nothing.

Four states

Normal and alarm are the two resistances. A short (0 Ω) or a cut wire (open) is reported as a trouble or tamper.

Tight pairs

Pairs where alarm is less than 1.5× normal are flagged. They leave little margin for long runs, tolerance and temperature.

Voltages assume the input in the REX concept board: pull-up to V, series resistor to the terminal, loop to GND. Change the values to match any input.
